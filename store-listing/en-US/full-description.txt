Every key does more.

Calcula keeps everyday arithmetic simple and puts scientific functions right under your thumb. Flick a digit up or down for sin, cos, log, squares, roots and more. The functions are named on the keys, so you can find them without hunting through menus.

CALCULATE WITH CONFIDENCE
See answers as you type. Work with exact decimal arithmetic, smart percentages and simple fractions. Edit an expression like text, move the cursor, and undo or redo a change.

KEEP THE WORK THAT MATTERS
Your calculation history stays above the keys. Reuse a result, add a note, or share your calculations as text. On a tablet or a larger screen, history has its own pane beside the keypad.

SEE THE MATH
Plot up to six functions and equations. Explore parameters with sliders and inspect roots and turning points. Keep recent graphs and share a graph as an image.

CONVERT WITHOUT STARTING OVER
Convert length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data, energy and pressure. Bring a number from the calculator into the converter, then take the converted answer back.

MAKE THE KEYPAD YOURS
Choose light or dark mode, color families, or supported wallpaper colors. Preview four color styles, resize the keys, and show function hints as names, dots or nothing. Reassign keys and flick shortcuts, save your own functions, and use scientific constants and named memories.

GO FURTHER WHEN YOU NEED TO
Use an optional RPN stack, scientific or engineering notation, and memory keys. Programmer mode works in hexadecimal, decimal, octal and binary, with bitwise operations and tappable bits. Statistics gives you the mean, median, standard deviations and range of a list.

AT HOME ON YOUR SCREEN
A responsive layout for phones, tablets and larger windows. An Android home-screen widget puts the calculator within reach.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your calculations stay on your device.
