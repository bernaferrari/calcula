# Privacy Policy for Calcula

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

Calcula is an offline calculator developed by **Bernardo Ferrari**. This policy covers the Calcula Android app, package `com.bernaferrari.calcula`.

## The short version

**We do not collect your personal data, calculations, or app usage data.** Calcula has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Calculations are processed on your device. The Android app does not request Internet access and does not send your data to the developer or a collection server.

## What stays on your device

Calcula saves the information needed to make its features work, including:

- Calculation history and any notes you add.
- Saved graphs, functions, constants, memories, and statistics entries.
- Appearance settings, key layouts, and other preferences.
- Local key-use counts and totals used for the optional “Your Calcula” summary.
- Data needed to display the home-screen calculator widget.

These records are stored in the app’s private storage. Local usage counts are for your own summary; they are not analytics sent to us. We do not have access to your device’s stored Calcula data.

## Sharing and exports are your choice

Calcula can copy, export, or share calculations, graphs, layouts, and summary images when you ask it to. Content you put on the clipboard or send to another app is handled by your operating system and the app or recipient you choose. Calcula does not automatically upload this content or send us a copy.

## Device backups

Calcula allows Android’s system backup and restore features. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your backup provider may include app data in a backup or transfer it to a new device. These features are controlled by your operating system and provider, under their own privacy policies. The developer cannot access those backups. You can review or disable them in your device’s backup settings.

## Your control and data retention

You can delete calculations and reset your local usage summary within the app. To remove all local app data, clear Calcula’s storage in Android’s app settings or uninstall the app. Stored history may also be trimmed as part of the app’s storage limits.

System backups are managed separately and may restore data after a reinstall. Manage any backup copies through your device or backup provider. There is no developer-held account or collection database to delete.

## Permissions and other services

Calcula does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. It uses vibration for optional key feedback. Android components used by the widget may also declare ordinary system permissions for scheduling and updating the widget; they are not used to collect or transmit your data.

Google Play, Android, your backup provider, and any browser or app you choose to open may process information independently under their own policies. This policy describes Calcula’s behavior, not those separate services.

## If you contact us

If you send feedback, email, or open a GitHub issue, we receive the information you choose to include so that we can respond. Do not include private calculations or other sensitive information unless you want to share it. GitHub issues are public. Support correspondence is used to respond and resolve the issue, not for advertising; you can request deletion of correspondence held by the developer by email.

## Children

Calcula has no accounts or tracking and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

If Calcula’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. Any future collection of data would need to be disclosed in the policy and the app’s store information.
